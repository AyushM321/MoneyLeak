MONEYLEAK ANDROID APK — BEGINNER STEPS

1. Download and EXTRACT this ZIP. Do not upload the ZIP itself.
2. Open https://github.com/AyushM321/MoneyLeak in your browser.
3. Upload the extracted files/folders to the repository root, preserving paths, and commit to the default branch (main/master). Keep existing app files.
4. Open Settings → Actions → General → Workflow permissions. Choose Read and write permissions and save.
5. Open Actions → “MoneyLeak Android APK and Auto Version”. Wait until the run completes successfully.
6. Open the run and download the APK from the Artifacts section. Extract the downloaded artifact ZIP to get the .apk.
7. Transfer the APK to your Android phone and tap it to install.

VERSION RULE: each update bundle supplied for MoneyLeak has its version incremented (v0.3 → v0.4 → v0.5 → ...). The Android workflow builds the exact version in version.json and does not accidentally bump it a second time.
This first build is a debug APK. It is for personal installation, not Google Play publishing. New APK builds must be downloaded and installed manually; the website cannot silently update a native APK.
