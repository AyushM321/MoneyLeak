Privacy policy

MoneyLeak processes statement files in your browser. It does not intentionally upload your statement transactions to a MoneyLeak server. Parsed transaction data used by Statement history is stored in this browser's local storage on this device.

  • Your saved history is limited to this browser profile and device.
  • Clearing browser site data may delete saved history.
  • Use Clear history to remove saved statement records from this device.
  • PDF.js may be loaded from a public CDN, which requires an internet connection.
  • Never upload banking passwords, PINs, OTPs, or other authentication secrets.

MoneyLeak is a personal finance insights tool. Check all imported dates, amounts, categories, and calculations yourself before relying on them.
